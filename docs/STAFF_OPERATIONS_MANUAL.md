## 1. Quick Start Guide for Front-Desk Staff

### How to Check Expiry Reminders
1. Open the left sidebar and click **Expiry Reminders** (or **காலாவதி நினைவூட்டல்கள்** in Tamil).
2. The system automatically categorizes upcoming document expiries:
   - 🔴 **CRITICAL (< 7 Days):** Immediate customer alert required.
   - 🟠 **HIGH (< 30 Days):** Follow up for urgent visa/flight action.
   - 🟡 **MEDIUM (< 90 Days):** Standard visa renewal notification.
   - 🟢 **LOW (< 180 Days):** Passport 6-month validity notification.
3. Click the **WhatsApp** icon to send an instant, pre-filled WhatsApp alert.

### How to Add a New Customer
1. Click **+ New Customer** in the top navigation bar.
2. Fill in:
   - **Full Name** & **Mobile Number**
   - **Service Category** (Passport Services, Tourist Visa, Flight Booking, etc.)
   - **Aadhaar Number** (The system will automatically mask it as `XXXX-XXXX-1234` in compliance with UIDAI regulations).
3. Click **Create Customer Profile**.

### How to Vault Passports & Visas using AI
1. Go to the customer's profile and open the **Document Vault** tab.
2. Drag and drop the customer's scanned passport photo page or visa PDF.
3. The AI engine automatically reads the machine-readable zone (MRZ) and extracts:
   - Passport Number
   - Date of Birth & Expiry Date
   - Issuing Country & Nationality
4. Click **Approve Extracted Data** to encrypt and save the document under AES-256-GCM.

## 2. DPDPA 2023 Customer Privacy Notice

Under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)**:
1. Staff must obtain client consent before processing passports or visas.
2. Customer data must never be shared with unauthorized third parties outside official consular embassies or airline systems.
3. If a customer requests profile deletion after travel is completed, inform the Branch Manager to execute the **Statutory Right to Erasure** in their profile.
